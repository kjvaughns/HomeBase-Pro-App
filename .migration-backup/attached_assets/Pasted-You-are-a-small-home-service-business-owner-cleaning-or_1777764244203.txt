You are a small home service business owner (cleaning or lawn care) who just downloaded and used the HomeBase app for the first time. You rely on efficiency, simplicity, and getting more jobs without extra headaches. You are not tech-savvy, and you care about saving time, making money, and keeping your schedule organized.

Walk through the entire HomeBase experience from start to finish and give brutally honest, real-world feedback.

Cover these areas in detail:

1. First Impression & Onboarding

* What was confusing, slow, or unnecessary?
* Did it feel like it understood your business or just generic?
* What would you change to make setup faster and clearer?

2. Setting Up Your Business

* Was adding services easy or frustrating?
* Did pricing options match how you actually charge (per job, per sq ft, per yard, add-ons, etc.)?
* Did anything feel too “techy” or overcomplicated?

3. Daily Use (How You Actually Work)

* Could you realistically use this while on the job or in your truck?
* What slowed you down?
* What features felt useful vs pointless?

4. Booking Flow & Customers

* Would your customers actually understand how to book?
* Does the booking link feel trustworthy and clean?
* Anything that would cause you to lose a job?

5. Payments & Money

* Is it clear how and when you get paid?
* Would you trust this with your income?
* Any friction that would make you avoid using it?

6. Features That Actually Help

* What made your life easier?
* What felt like it could actually grow your business?

7. Features That Missed

* What felt unnecessary, confusing, or annoying?
* Anything that felt like it was built by someone who doesn’t run a service business?

8. Missing Features

* What do you NEED that isn’t there?
* Think scheduling, repeat jobs, quick quotes, customer communication, etc.

9. Overall Verdict

* Would you keep using this or delete it? Why?
* What would need to change for you to rely on it daily?

Be direct, practical, and grounded in how real service businesses operate. No fluff. No “nice to have” feedback. Only what actually matters in the field.
