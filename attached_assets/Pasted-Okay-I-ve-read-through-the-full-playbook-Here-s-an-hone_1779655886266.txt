The Plays That Matter Most For HomeBase Right Now

1 — Time To Value (Most Critical)

The playbook defines this as the gap between signup and experiencing real benefit. For HomeBase this is everything.

Right now your Time To Value sequence is:

	•	Provider signs up
	•	Sets up profile
	•	Creates booking link
	•	Sends link to first client
	•	Client books and pays
	•	Provider gets paid
	•	THAT is your Time to Value moment

The faster you compress that sequence the higher your activation rate. The playbook recommends targeting under 5 minutes from signup to first core action.

What to do: Redesign your onboarding as a guided checklist that walks providers through those exact steps immediately after signup. Don’t let them explore the app freely — guide them directly to their first booking link creation. Every extra step before that moment loses providers.

2 — Success Moments

The playbook defines these as rewards immediately after completing a key action. This is where HomeBase has a massive untapped opportunity.

When a provider gets their first payment through HomeBase — what happens? Just a notification? That’s not enough.

What to do: Build a dedicated success screen that triggers the first time a provider receives a payment through HomeBase. Full screen moment. Confetti animation. Their earnings displayed prominently. A message like “Your first HomeBase payment. This is just the beginning.”

That moment needs to feel like a milestone — because it is. It’s the exact moment your free trial converts to a paying subscriber. Make it unforgettable and they’ll tell other providers about it.

3 — Commitment

The playbook says users who declare intent stick around longer. Duolingo does this by asking “what’s your daily learning goal?” before you start.

What to do: During provider onboarding ask one question: “How much do you want to earn through HomeBase this month?” Let them set a goal. Then show a progress bar toward that goal on their dashboard. Send a push notification when they hit 50% and 100%.

A provider who has set a $5,000 monthly goal and is at $3,200 is not cancelling their subscription. They’re too close to their own target.

4 — Effort Moat

The playbook defines this as turning invested effort into loyalty. The more a provider builds inside HomeBase the harder it is to leave.

HomeBase already has this built in — client list, recurring jobs, booking links, service history. But you need to make providers aware of what they’ve built.

What to do: Show providers a monthly summary — “This month you managed 47 jobs, served 12 clients, and processed $4,200 through HomeBase.” That summary makes the effort visible. Leaving now means losing all of that context.

5 — Variable Reward

The playbook describes this as unpredictability that drives return behavior. It’s why slot machines are addictive.

What to do: Show providers something slightly different every time they open the app. Rotate between:

	•	A new homeowner in their area searching for their service type
	•	A tip about optimizing their most popular service
	•	A milestone they’re close to hitting
	•	A stat about how their profile is performing

That unpredictability makes providers open the app daily to see what’s new — even on days they have no scheduled jobs.

6 — The Paywall Play

The playbook has a specific framework for turning conversion friction into product desire. Your current paywall is already strong — free until first job is exactly right. But the paywall moment itself could be enhanced.

When the subscription trigger hits — the first completed job — instead of just showing a payment prompt, show:

“You just earned [X] through HomeBase. Subscribe to keep your business running on autopilot.”

Connect the subscription moment directly to the value they just experienced. That framing converts at a fundamentally different rate than a generic “start your subscription” prompt.

7 — Gamified Progress

Streak mechanics, progress bars, milestone badges. The playbook shows these drive habit formation more than almost anything else.

What to do for providers:

	•	Jobs completed this month vs last month
	•	Days with at least one booking — create a streak
	•	Milestone badges — “First $1,000”, “10 Clients”, “First Recurring Job”
	•	A leaderboard showing top providers in their city or category (opt-in)

What to do for homeowners:

	•	Maintenance completion percentage for their home
	•	Streak of services kept up to date
	•	Home Health Score improving over time as they complete maintenance tasks

8 — Referral (Designed Into The Product)

The playbook’s referral framework is clear — referral should be triggered at success moments not randomly. The right time to ask a provider to refer someone is immediately after they receive their first payment or hit a revenue milestone.

“You just hit $1,000 through HomeBase. Know another service pro who’d love this? Give them a month free.”

That’s the exact moment they’re feeling the value most acutely. That’s when they share.

The Three You Should Build First

If I had to prioritize based on your current stage:

1 — Time to Value optimization — redesign onboarding as a guided 5-step checklist. This directly impacts activation rate which is your most important metric right now.

2 — Success Moments on first payment — build the full-screen celebration moment for first payment received. This is your paywall conversion trigger and your most powerful word-of-mouth generator simultaneously.

3 — Commitment goal setting — add the monthly earnings goal question to onboarding. One question. One number. Dramatically reduces churn.

Those three changes alone could move your activation rate from whatever it is now to significantly higher — which at scale means thousands of dollars more in monthly revenue from the same number of signups.
